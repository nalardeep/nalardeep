With great power comes great responsibility.

## The Copyright Problem
If an AI is trained on copyrighted art, is the output stolen? Courts are still deciding.

### Deepfakes and Truth
We are entering an era where seeing is no longer believing.
- **Political Disinformation**: AI video can sway elections.
- **Identity Theft**: Voice cloning scams are on the rise.

## Solutions
- **Watermarking**: embedding invisible signatures in AI content.
- **Provenance**: Tracking the origin of digital assets (C2PA).

We must build safety rails before the train leaves the station.

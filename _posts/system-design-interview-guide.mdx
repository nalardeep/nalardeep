System design interviews are the hurdle between you and a Senior Engineer role.

## The framework
1. **Requirements**: Functional vs Non-functional.
2. **Back-of-envelope Math**: Estimate traffic and storage needs.
3. **High-level Design**: Draw the boxes (LB, App Server, DB).

## Key Concepts
- **Horizontal Scaling**: Adding more machines.
- **Sharding**: Splitting your database.
- **Caching**: Redis is your best friend.
- **CAP Theorem**: Consistency, Availability, Partition Tolerance.

## Example: Designing Twitter
Focus on the "Fan-out" problem. When Justin Bieber tweets, how do we deliver it to 100M followers instantly?

We are witnessing a shift from passive AI chatbots to active AI agents.

## What is Agentic AI?
Unlike LLMs that simply predict text, agents have "tools" and "goals". They can browse the web, run code, and file tickets.

### key Components
1. **Planning**: Breaking down complex goals.
2. **Memory**: Remembering context over long periods.
3. **Tools**: APIs, databases, and browsers.

## Use Cases
- **Coding Assistants**: Writing and debugging entire features.
- **Personal Shoppers**: Finding deals and booking flights.

## The Future
As models get smarter, agents will become our primary interface for digital work.
